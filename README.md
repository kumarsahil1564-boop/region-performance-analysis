# Region Performance Analysis - Superstore

## 📌 Project Overview

Task 18: Compare sales and profit across different regions using the Superstore dataset.

This project analyzes regional sales performance, total profit, and profit margin to understand how different regions contribute to overall business performance.

## 🎯 Objective

- Compare total sales across regions
- Compare total profit across regions
- Calculate profit margin
- Rank regions based on sales and profit
- Create visualizations for regional comparison

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Excel
- Google Sheets
- Power BI
- GitHub

## 📊 Regional Performance

| Region | Total Sales | Total Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| West | 725,457.82 | 108,418.45 | 14.94% |
| East | 678,781.24 | 91,522.78 | 13.49% |
| Central | 501,239.89 | 39,706.36 | 7.92% |
| South | 391,721.91 | 46,749.43 | 11.93% |

## 🔍 Key Findings

- West recorded the highest total sales.
- West also recorded the highest total profit.
- South recorded the lowest total sales.
- Central recorded the lowest total profit.
- West had the highest profit margin among the four regions.
- Regional comparison helps identify differences in sales and profitability.
